A Letter For Nayab

Happy Birthday, Nayab

Today is your birthday, but this isn't just a birthday wish.
It's a thank you.
A thank you for entering my life in a way I never expected.

On 30 March 2026, I randomly added a girl on Snapchat.
At the time, it felt completely ordinary.
Just another request. Just another conversation.
But life has a strange habit of hiding important moments inside ordinary ones.

From the first conversations, there was something different about you.
You weren't someone I talked to because I was bored.
You became someone I wanted to talk to because your presence made every day feel lighter.

Then came the day we exchanged pictures.
And honestly?
The guy sitting in Karachi suddenly found himself looking at the most beautiful girl from Lahore.

The funny thing is, I never used to like Lahoris.
Yet somehow one person completely changed that opinion.
Turns out all it takes is meeting the right person.

What I admire most about you is how much you care.
You often pretend not to. You act unbothered.
But I've seen the version of you that remembers details.
The version that worries quietly.
The version that gives more than she ever admits.

And that is one of the most beautiful things about you.

Before you, I never understood how people could spend hours on calls.
Now somehow hours disappear when we're talking.
Late nights stop feeling late.
Silence becomes comfortable. Time becomes softer.

Somewhere along the way, you stopped being someone I talked to.
You became someone I looked forward to.
Then you became a habit. Then peace.

And whether you realize it or not, you made life brighter.
You made me laugh more. Smile more. Feel lighter.

That's a gift. And I don't think I'll ever forget it.

So today I hope life gives something back to you.
I hope your dreams arrive. I hope happiness stays longer.
I hope the difficult days become easier.
And I hope every beautiful thing in life finds its way to you.

Because if anyone deserves beautiful things, it's you.

Happy Birthday, Nayab 💖
